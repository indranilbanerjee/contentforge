# Hospital AI Adoption Survey 2026

Published by the Regional Health Informatics Council.

Of 412 hospitals surveyed in spring 2026, 38% used at least one AI tool in diagnostic imaging, up from 22% in 2024. Radiology departments led adoption; pathology followed at 17%. Respondents named staff training (61%) and data-sharing agreements (44%) as the main barriers.
